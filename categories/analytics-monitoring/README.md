## Stack Recommendations

### Early-Stage Product Team
1. **Analytics:** PostHog (all-in-one)
2. **Feature Flags:** PostHog or Statsig

### Growth-Stage Product
1. **Product Analytics:** Mixpanel or Amplitude
2. **CDP:** Segment
3. **Experimentation:** Statsig

### DevOps/Platform Team
1. **Monitoring:** Grafana or Datadog
2. **Product:** PostHog

### Data-Intensive Application
1. **CDP:** Segment
2. **Pipelines:** Estuary Flow
3. **Analytics:** Amplitude
