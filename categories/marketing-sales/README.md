## Use Case Recommendations

### B2B SaaS
1. **CRM:** HubSpot or Attio
2. **Sales Intel:** Apollo
3. **Email:** Customer.io

### B2C Product
1. **Marketing:** Netcore Cloud
2. **Email:** Brevo or Customer.io
3. **Ads:** Google Ads + Reddit

### Early-Stage (No Budget)
1. **CRM:** HubSpot Free + Tier 3 discount
2. **Email:** Brevo Startup
3. **Ads:** Google Ads credit

### Enterprise Sales
1. **CRM:** HubSpot Tier 2
2. **Sales Intel:** Apollo
3. **Outreach:** Custom

## HubSpot Tier Decision Tree

```
Raised $0-$2M?
├── Yes → Tier 3 (75% off Year 1)
└── No
    └── Raised $2M+, Pre-Series B?
        ├── Yes → Tier 2 (50% off Year 1)
        └── No → Tier 1 (30% off Year 1)
            └── Requires partner affiliation
```
