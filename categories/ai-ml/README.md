## Nebius

### Three-Tier Program for AI Startups
| Tier | Credits | Inference Credits | Target Stage |
|------|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| AI Explorer | $5,000 | - | Bootstrapped/Pre-Seed |
| AI Builder | $100,000 | $6,000 | Pre-Seed/Seed |
| AI Scaler | $150,000 | $10,000 | Seed to Pre-Series B |

- **Duration:** 12 months
- **Key Benefits:**
  - Priority access to NVIDIA GPUs
  - Dedicated support (higher tiers)
  - Co-branding requirements
- **Requirements:**
  - AI-first product beyond prototype
  - Founded within 5-7 years
  - Team under 8 employees
  - Partner affiliation (higher tiers)
- **Timeline:** 7-10 business days decision
- **Link:** [nebius.com/startups-program](https://nebius.com/startups-program)
